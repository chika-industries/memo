### Scene : Bond as Safe Havens

**Setting:** Jerry’s apartment. Jerry is on the couch. Kramer enters wearing a life preserver over a blazer and holding a crisp bond certificate.

**Kramer:** Jerry! I’m a safe haven!

**Jerry:** You look like a lifeguard who got fired from an accounting firm.

**Kramer:** No! I’m talking about bonds! When the stock market gets choppy, money runs to bonds like me to a free buffet! It’s a flight to quality!

**Jerry:** A flight to quality in a life preserver?

**Kramer:** Exactly! A bond is a loan, Jerry! You give the government a thousand dollars, they pay you a little coupon every six months, and at maturity they give your thousand bucks back. It’s an anchor! A lifeboat! A snuggle blanket made of interest payments!

**Jerry:** A snuggle blanket that loses value when interest rates go up.

**Kramer:** Rates? Who cares about rates? It’s safe!

**Jerry:** If new bonds start paying more, your old bond paying less is worth less. Bond prices and yields go opposite ways. You’re not in a lifeboat — you’re on a seesaw.

**Kramer:** Fine, fine, but I’ll just hold it to maturity!

**Jerry:** Then you get your principal back in dollars that buy less. You’re safe from default, not from inflation. And if it’s a corporate bond, you’ve got credit risk too.

**Kramer:** Credit risk? I bought a junk bond!

**Jerry:** A junk bond is not a safe haven. That’s a stock market in a tuxedo.

**Kramer:** But Treasuries are backed by the full faith and credit of the U.S. government!

**Jerry:** The same government that can’t pass a budget? That’s your safe haven?

**Kramer:** It’s relative, Jerry! Relative! When stocks are doing the hully-gully, high-quality bonds are the calm cousin. It’s not “no risk” — it’s “less freak-out risk”!

**Jerry:** So you’re diversifying?

**Kramer:** Absolutely! Sixty percent stocks, forty percent life preserver!

**Jerry:** That’s not a portfolio. That’s a pool party.
